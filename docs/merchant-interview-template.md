# Merchant Interview Template

Use this to collect real validation without fake traction claims.

1. Would you pay only after a verified visit or purchase?
2. What fraud do you fear in referral or coupon programs?
3. Would terminal + customer confirmation be acceptable at your counter?
4. What payout size makes sense for one verified conversion?
5. What would stop you from using this?
6. Would a privacy-preserving proof passport help you show activity to partners, lenders, sponsors, or community programs?

Evidence slot types:

- `merchant_quote`
- `merchant_demo_completed`
- `merchant_objection`
- `merchant_price_signal`
- `merchant_followup_interest`

Do not invent merchant traction. Empty evidence is better than fake evidence.
