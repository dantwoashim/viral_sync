# Day 88 Campaign Templates

## Templates

- Cafe: 3 confirmed friends unlock coffee credit.
- QSR: 2 confirmed friends unlock a table upgrade.
- Hostel: 3 verified guests unlock hostel cafe credit.
- Creator: 5 verified audience visits unlock a creator perk.

## App Surface

Open `/merchant/templates`.

## Use

Operators can copy the template into `/merchant/campaigns` and publish without adding new code.
