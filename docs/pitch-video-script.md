# Pitch Video Script

## 0:00-0:15 - Hook

Online referral systems can be faked. Offline conversions are worse. Viral Sync turns counter-attested conversions into Solana-verifiable proof objects.

## 0:15-0:40 - Problem

Merchants pay platforms for clicks and coupons, but the platform controls attribution. There is no open settlement layer for counter-attested conversions.

## 0:40-1:30 - Demo

Show campaign funding, claim-pass account, terminal confirmation, visitor signature, receipt recording, settlement, proof page, and passport.

## 1:30-2:05 - Fraud Gauntlet

Merchant-only receipt: blocked. Wrong terminal: blocked. Visitor mismatch: blocked. Duplicate replay: blocked. Wrong beneficiary: blocked.

## 2:05-2:35 - Market

This starts with local merchants and events. The bigger market is permissionless CPA: any merchant posts a conversion bounty; any creator, agent, or referrer routes demand; Solana settles only after counter-attestation.

## 2:35-3:00 - Close

Viral Sync is proof-of-outcome infrastructure for Solana commerce: merchants escrow rewards, enrolled terminals and visitors counter-attest conversions, and payouts settle only when the POC-1 receipt verifies.
