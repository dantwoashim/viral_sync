# Staff Guide

## One-Sentence Script

"Show me your Viral Sync QR or code; I will confirm it once you are here at the counter."

## Live Flow

1. Open `/merchant/scan`.
2. Scan the QR or type the six-character code.
3. Check that the challenge is live.
4. Confirm once.
5. Tell the customer the reward has been recorded.

## Training Flow

Use `/merchant/training` before the shift. Training mode uses fake data and does not create rewards or receipts.

## Do Not

- Do not confirm a screenshot for someone who is not present.
- Do not confirm the same code twice.
- Do not share the manager PIN with floor staff.
- Do not promise cash payouts during the pilot.
