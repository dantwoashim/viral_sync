# Day 85 Funnel Leak Analysis

## Funnel

1. Invite to claim.
2. Claim to visit.
3. Visit to confirm.
4. Confirm to receipt.

## Product Fixes

- Invite to claim: richer WhatsApp, SMS, and native share copy.
- Claim to visit: clear redemption windows and visible counter instructions.
- Visit to confirm: larger staff terminal controls and automatic code formatting.
- Confirm to receipt: receipt explorer and outbox monitoring.
