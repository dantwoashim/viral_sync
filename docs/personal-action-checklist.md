# Personal Action Checklist

These items cannot be honestly completed by code alone.

## Submission Assets

- Record the final 90-120 second demo video from the working app.
- Add captions/callouts in the video editor.
- Upload the video to Loom or YouTube and verify the public link.
- Take final screenshots from the live or local app routes.
- Verify every final public link from another browser/session.

## Merchant And Traction Proof

- Get explicit merchant permission before using names, quotes, logos, or identifiable screenshots publicly.
- Replace `Permission pending` testimonial placeholders only after approval.
- Confirm any paid pilot/LOI status with the actual merchant owner.
- Do not count unverified claims as revenue or paid traction.

## Security And Launch Readiness

- Arrange external review/audit before claiming mainnet or uncapped funds readiness.
- Replace temporary staff PIN flow with production RBAC/multisig/admin controls before broad beta.
- Configure production Postgres, backups, monitoring, alert delivery, and hosted relayer secrets.
- Confirm Solana program deployment, IDL, upgrade authority, and environment snapshot for the exact public demo.

## Final Submission

- Fill the hackathon submission form.
- Confirm submission receipt.
- Archive the submitted video link, repo URL, demo URL, write-up, and screenshots.
- Keep limitations visible: capped beta, not externally audited, local JSON is dev-only, scanner/POS integrations are pilot paths.
